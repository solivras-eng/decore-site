Frequently asked questions

All answers

Capacity, access, conditions

Find here the answers to our most frequently asked questions about the venue, equipment and booking conditions. For anything specific to your project, contact us directly.

The venue

Capacity & spaces

What is the capacity of the loft?

The loft accommodates up to ~70 people standing (cocktail), ~30 to 40 seated (dinner or seminar) and up to ~50 for a half-day event. The La Jolie Maison annex (~70 m²) can be added as an option for larger groups or distinct spaces.

What is the total floor area?

The main loft is approximately 300 m². The annex adds 70 m² more. The garden and tree-lined courtyard (enclosed plot) are included and available for outdoor events in fine weather.

Is the venue accessible for people with reduced mobility?

The main space is on a single level and step-free. Some areas (mezzanine, garden) involve different levels. Please mention any accessibility requirements when enquiring — we will do our best to accommodate them.

Access & getting there

Finding us

Where is the loft and how do I get there?

18 rue Édouard Vaillant, 93170 Bagnolet — 50 m from Porte de Montreuil (Paris 20th). Metro line 9, stations Porte de Montreuil or Robespierre, approximately 5 minutes on foot. Also reachable from Gallieni station (line 3) in about 15 minutes on foot.

Is there parking on site or nearby?

There is no private parking on site. Several public pay-and-display car parks are within 5 minutes' walk. Given the direct metro access (line 9), we strongly recommend public transport.

Hours & music

Timing and constraints

Until what time can events run?

Events can run from 8 am to 1 am, 7 days a week. For evening events, guests must leave by 1 am.

Is amplified music (DJ, band) permitted?

Yes. Amplified music (DJ, live band, playlist) is permitted until midnight. The loft has a professional sound system. After midnight, only soft background music is allowed. Please mention your music programme in your enquiry.

Caterers & equipment

Suppliers and technology

Can we bring our own caterer or external suppliers?

Yes, external suppliers (caterer, DJ, photographer, florist) are welcome subject to prior agreement. We can recommend our partner caterers, but you are free to bring your own contacts.

Is the kitchen accessible and well equipped?

Yes. The loft has a fully equipped kitchen (oven, refrigerator, large worktop, sink) accessible to caterers or for on-site preparation. It is suitable for professional catering.

What technical equipment is available?

The loft provides: professional sound system, 4K projector and screen, wireless microphone on request, high-speed Wi-Fi and adjustable lighting. Additional furniture (tables, chairs, lounge seating) can be hired as an option. For film shoots and productions, early access and full logistical support can be arranged.

Booking

Visits and quotes

Can we visit the loft before booking?

Absolutely — we strongly encourage a visit. The atmosphere of the loft is best felt in person, far more than in photos. Email us at contact@agnesbouche.com to arrange a viewing at a time that suits you.

How do I request a quote and how quickly will I receive a reply?

Fill in the contact form or email contact@agnesbouche.com. Please include: preferred date, type of event, estimated number of guests and specific requirements. We reply within 48 hours with a personalised, no-obligation quote.

What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation terms are detailed in the hire agreement issued at the time of booking. We try to be as flexible as possible in unforeseen circumstances — please contact us as soon as possible if your plans change.
