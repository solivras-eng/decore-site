Privacy policy

Last updated: May 2026. This policy describes how Le Décor à l'Envers processes personal data collected through the website decoralenvers.com, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable French law.

1. Data controller

The controller is Le Décor à l'Envers, operated by Agnès Bouche, rue Édouard Vaillant.

Privacy contact: contact@agnesbouche.com

SIREN, SIRET, legal form and VAT details are listed on the legal notice (or on your quote and hire agreement).

2. Data collected and purposes

Contact / quote request form (contact pages, FR/EN): name, first name, email address, phone where provided, event type, dates, free-text message. Purposes: respond to your request, prepare a quote, arrange a visit or callback, manage the commercial relationship prior to any contract.

Transmission via Formspree: when you click send, form fields are transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our provider Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States), which forwards the message to our business inbox. This static website does not store form submissions on a server we operate; the technical sending is therefore handled by Formspree on our behalf.

Browsing: technical data exchanged with the server (IP address, timestamp, browser type) for security, correct operation of the site and minimal technical logging.

Legal bases: pre-contractual measures and legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) for form requests; legitimate interest for security and service stability.

3. Recipients and processors

Formspree (processor — contact form): Formspree, Inc. (United States) receives and processes data entered in the contact form in order to deliver it to us (message routing, temporary or longer retention depending on their service, spam prevention). Formspree acts as a processor under the GDPR for this purpose; we remain the controller towards you.

Transfers outside the EEA: this data may be hosted or accessed in the United States. Transfers rely, as applicable, on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (Article 46 GDPR) and/or safeguards described by Formspree. Further information: Formspree privacy policy and formspree.io.

You may contact us at contact@agnesbouche.com for details on Formspree and transfer safeguards.

Site hosting (HTML, images) is provided by Vercel Inc. (United States); technical logs may be kept under their policy.

We do not sell your data to third parties for marketing.

4. Retention

Messages and contact details relating to a commercial request are kept for as long as needed to handle the request, then for any statutory limitation period applicable to commercial matters or for the duration set out in any signed contract (adjust according to your internal policy and legal advice).

Hosting logs are retained according to the host’s practices.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

This site loads fonts via Google Fonts; Google may receive technical data (IP address, user agent). You can limit tracking through your browser settings. If your legal review requires it, add a consent banner or self-host fonts.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration (HTTPS, reputable providers). No system is perfectly secure.

7. Your rights

Where GDPR applies you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability in the situations provided by law.

To exercise your rights: contact@agnesbouche.com. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French supervisory authority).

8. Children

The site is not directed at collecting data from children under 16 without appropriate parental consent where the law requires it.
