The Artist

Agnès Visual artist · Paris
Career

A life devoted to creation

Agnès Bouche Barbare lives and works in Paris. She creates stunning original works from a unique and subtle combination of wood, oil and pigments. Each piece originates from a poem, a quote or a sensation — this message vector, echoing the visual, opens a second layer of meaning. The interplay of intersections and the juxtaposition of colours, paired with the sobriety of abstraction, generate a rhythm, an almost musical movement.

After passing her entrance exam at the Fine Arts School of Paris, she attended sculpture classes at the École Boulle — where wood became her favourite material — and studied Art History at the École du Louvre and plastic arts at the Charpentier Academy. She travelled throughout Europe for twelve years, residing primarily in Italy and Denmark, her practice shaped as much by French creation as by the rigour of Nordic design.

Back in France, she dedicated herself exclusively to painting, joined the Maison des Artistes and became, in 2009, a member of the Ateliers d'Art de France. Selected in 2013 for the exhibition « Revelations » at the Grand Palais, she showed twenty of her works at the Palais de Tokyo in 2014.

The studio — Paris

The Material

Wood, pigments & relief

With a depth of 2 inches, Agnès's works merge painting and sculpture, ranging in size from 16 to over 120 inches. Made from wood chosen among different species — oak, beech, pine — each piece is cut, sanded and transformed before receiving successive layers of pigments and mediums.

The pictorial matter becomes carnal: it is through abandonment and control, the defined and the ethereal, that her hands find their way. Matte and glossy, shadows and light respond to one another, transmitting the impulse to movement.

Artistic approach

Intentions

Writing & double reading

Each work originates from a poem, a quote or a text written by the artist. Echoing the paintings, these thoughts open a second layer of meaning. Ideograms, letters and words from diverse origins — Sanskrit, Japanese, Hebrew, Hindi, Greek, Arabic, Chinese, Persian — are juxtaposed to create an imaginary ethnic bond that poetically unifies diverse peoples and their spiritual approaches.

The human machine

Fascinated by the magnificent « human machine » that we are, Agnès explores multiple paths whose themes are experienced as beautiful, necessary stages traversed through the thread of her personal history. She is guided by a communion between abandonment and control, defined and ethereal, relief and impalpable — setting matte and glossy, shadows and light in dialogue.

Energy & movement

These contrasts transmit the impulse to movement, like feminine energy responding as a pendulum to the purely masculine. The result is an artistic work intimately linked to the senses — from sight to touch, between imagination and sensuality — where the suggested rhythm becomes a virtually musical bond. Music remains omnipresent and plays a preponderant role in terms of inspiration.

Transformation & sharing

From this labyrinth of thoughts and sensations arises the idea of transformation favoured by movement, in a perspective of progression, elevation, union and sharing. Agnès believes that every work of art contains, in a very subtle way, a consequential energetic potential. Creating in full consciousness carries within itself the spark of authenticity.

In her own words

A personal message

« Listen beyond the music, perceive beyond the words. Until you feel beyond all possible interpretation. »

— Agnès Bouche Barbare

Following many rich inner experiences, I seek to evoke that force of the universe that has carried me ceaselessly. I have always been able to draw on the significant trials of my life, thanks to this wholesome force that brings me back to being, to our essence. A sensation of fullness, which often escapes us, reminding us of the blindness caused by our limited and restrictive consciousness. It is in this spirit that I create and it is around this that I would like to transmit. « Persevere in your being » wrote Spinoza.

Being an artist is — BEING — in full awareness, letting oneself be guided by the moment. It is knowing how to seize simple, fleeting moments, like the sparks of childhood, those paths overflowing with colours that present themselves to us, whose original intensity our instinct captures. It is, in the same way as spirituality, a natural human function consisting in recognising this other dimension of ourselves.

In creating, I discover as if by magic my intentions emerging from my fingertips — somewhat like living the « mad impression of getting lost while finding oneself ». For setting out to discover this central axis, where our fears evaporate of their own accord, is like detecting one's own « centre of gravity ».

My painting is elaborated from my own writings or the sensations that a poem or a quote evoke in me. This message vector, echoing the pictorial aspect, induces a double reading where each person can find their own. « Writing is drawing a door on an impassable wall, and then opening it » — Christian Bobin.

In this subtle communication, far from the beaten path, passionately, I attempt to offer the reflections of my elation. A visible homage to the invisible, a hymn to the intensity of life, to infinity.
